# Accession agreement to the CitCom.ai Data Space

An organisation joins the CitCom.ai Data Space with this agreement. Its legal
representative fills it in, signs it and uploads it in the onboarding portal. The
agreement is concluded when the registration is approved (clause 6).

## Clauses

### 1. Parties

The parties are the organisation identified above (the **participant**) and the
**CitCom.ai consortium**, the governance authority of the data space
([R2.1](../rulebook/README.md#2-governance)). The **operator**, VRAIN of the Universitat
Politècnica de València ([R2.2](../rulebook/README.md#2-governance)), acts on behalf of
the governance authority to approve registrations.

### 2. Object

The participant joins the data space and accepts its **rulebook**, including the policy
classes, as the rules of its participation. The rulebook is published at
<https://citcomai-hub.github.io/rulebook/> and kept in
<https://github.com/CitComAI-Hub/citcom-ds-rulebook>.

### 3. The rulebook in force

This agreement refers to the rulebook **in force at each moment**, not to a fixed version.
The rulebook changes only as its rule R2.3 says: the governance authority approves the
change, and it is announced to participants at least **30 days** before it takes effect,
unless it fixes a security problem. A participant that does not accept a change can
leave the data space before it takes effect (clause 8).

If this agreement and the rulebook differ, the rulebook prevails.

### 4. What the participant undertakes

The participant:

- issues credentials only to the people and software it controls, revokes them when that
  stops being true, and does not share them with other organisations (R3.3, R9.2);
- keeps the contact above up to date (R3.2);
- as a provider: has the right to offer each dataset, including the permission of its
  data rights holders; gives each dataset its correct category; offers no personal or
  confidential data; and keeps the conformance evidence of its connector and data (R6,
  R8.7);
- as a consumer: uses data and services under the terms of each offering, that is, its
  policy class and any additional requirements of its provider (R5.3, R7);
- tells the operator and the affected providers of any security incident or
  vulnerability affecting the data space within **72 hours** (R9.1).

### 5. What the governance authority and the operator undertake

The governance authority and the operator:

- decide on the registration and, if they deny it, give the reasons (R4.6);
- treat every participant the same way, and the operator does not use its position to
  access other participants' data or services (R2.5);
- announce changes to the rulebook as clause 3 says;
- tell the participant of vulnerabilities in software it runs that they learn of (R9.3).

### 6. Effect

The agreement is concluded, and the participation begins, when the governance authority
or the operator on its behalf approves the registration in the onboarding portal. The
participant is then added to the trust anchor of the data space (R4.1).

### 7. Pilot, as is

The data space is a pilot. Data and services are offered as is, on a best-effort basis,
without availability or quality guarantees, unless an offering promises more (R1.3,
R10.1). Liability between participants follows the rulebook and the terms of each
offering, and otherwise the applicable law.

### 8. Leaving and exclusion

The participant can leave at any time by asking the operator. The governance authority
can warn, suspend or exclude a participant that seriously or repeatedly breaks the
rulebook (R4.2, R11.2). On leaving or exclusion the participant is removed from the trust
anchor and its purchases are cancelled (R4.3). Obligations on data already received, such
as attribution, confidentiality or deletion, continue after it leaves (R4.4).

### 9. Disputes

The parties first try to settle a dispute between themselves. If they cannot, the
governance authority decides (R11.1).

### 10. Personal data

The personal data in this agreement and in the onboarding portal (names and contact
details) is used only to manage the participation. Each party complies with the General
Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 when it processes personal data under this
agreement.

### 11. Duration

The agreement lasts until the participant leaves or is excluded.
